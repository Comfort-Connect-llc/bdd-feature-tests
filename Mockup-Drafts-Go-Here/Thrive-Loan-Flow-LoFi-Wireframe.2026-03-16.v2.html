Flow Overview — Promo Variation
The Thrive Promo 0% for 18 months loan flow. This variation requires the homeowner to upload a driver's license (front + back) before prequalification — the ID pre-fills the prequal form. The homeowner must also provide employer details during the application. All other phases follow the standard installment loan path.
