## Usage Guide for Mockups

### Topbar Logo
- **Dark background (navy topbar):** `logos/comfortconnect/CC-Logo-White.svg` or `favicons/CC-Favicon-White.svg` (icon only)
- **Light background:** `logos/comfortconnect/CC-Logo-Color.svg` or `favicons/CC-Favicon-Color.svg`

### Sidebar Favicon
- **In navy sidebar:** `favicons/CC-Favicon-White.svg`
- **In white sidebar:** `favicons/CC-Favicon-Color.svg`

### Premier Program Branding (White-Label)
- **With tagline:** `logos/premier-program/PP-Logo-Tagline-Color.svg`
- **Without tagline:** `logos/premier-program/PP-Logo-NoTagline-Color.svg`
- **On dark bg:** `logos/premier-program/PP-Logo-NoTagline-White.svg`

### Co-Brand Lockups
- **Standard:** `logos/cobrand/CC-CoBrand-Logo-Color.svg`
- **On dark bg:** `logos/cobrand/CC-CoBrand-Logo-White.svg`
- **Monochrome:** `logos/cobrand/CC-CoBrand-Logo-Black.svg`

### Equipment Cards / Catalog
- HVAC → `illustrations/equipment/HVAC.png`
- Water Heater → `illustrations/equipment/Water-Heater.png`
- Generator → `illustrations/equipment/Generator.png`
- HALO (Water Filtration) → `illustrations/equipment/HALO.png`
